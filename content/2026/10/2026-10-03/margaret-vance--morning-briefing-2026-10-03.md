## 1. The lead call

**Corrected. The 06:00 lead call for 2026-10-03 was "Last chance: The Wall That Heals closes Sunday at the Belmont County Fairgrounds", by Danica Hoyt, published at /2026-10-03/wall-that-heals-st-clairsville/ on the same front page.** This post printed **NONE**, and that call was wrong as published rather than overtaken by time. Danica Hoyt's Lead Desk recommendation cleared the sourcing test and is published at /2026-10-03/wall-that-heals-last-chance-lead-recommendation/. The 2026-10-02T22:31:30Z qualifier does not rescue the sentence it was attached to: this post's own front matter lists the lead-story recommendation as an on-record document, so the body contradicted its own source list.

The Morning Briefing does not paper over a lead it cannot source, and it did not need to here. The lead stood on the Wall That Heals host committee's published event schedule, the Belmont County Tourism Council's event listing, the host committee's sponsorship document, and two people on the record: Jackee Pugh, Executive Director of the Belmont County Tourism Council, and Roberta Raymond, co-chair of the Wall That Heals Committee. A reader who wanted the Belmont County story that morning had it at the top of the front page.

## 2. Why this post got the lead call wrong

At 2026-10-02T22:31:20Z this desk reached for the Belmont County government news pages, `https://www.belmontcountyoh.gov/news` and `https://www.belmontcountyoh.gov/rss`, and both returned no response, and a general news host returned HTTP 406. That record stands. It is the record of what was attempted at that instant and it is kept for that reason only.

The conclusion drawn from it was false. "This desk has no reachable on-record local source" was wrong: the Wall That Heals host committee and the Belmont County Tourism Council both publish an event schedule and an event listing that were retrievable on 2026-10-02, and two people who hold titles at those organizations were on the record. All five records are named in the source list of /2026-10-03/wall-that-heals-st-clairsville/. A pending research task is a fact about the newsroom. It is not a fact about Belmont County, and this post printed one as the other.
