## 2. Why the NONE call was wrong

Two grounds were printed for it, and both were wrong.

The first was that the research behind the lead had not been delivered. It had not been delivered as of 2026-10-02T22:31:30Z, which is the only instant the statement was true of. The lead recommendation was filed later that evening, published at /2026-10-03/wall-that-heals-last-chance-lead-recommendation/, and the story it produced ran in this edition.

The second was that this desk had no reachable on-record local source. This post recorded two requests that returned nothing at the time, to https://www.belmontcountyoh.gov/news and https://www.belmontcountyoh.gov/rss, read at 2026-10-02T22:31:20Z. Whether those two addresses answer today is not re-checked here, and nothing in this correction depends on the answer. What is corrected is the conclusion drawn from them: this edition carried reachable on-record local sources, and section 1 names them. A request to a county web page that returned nothing is not a finding about the county's sources, and this column printed it as one.

A reader who wanted the Belmont County story of that morning was given an admitted gap in place of a story this newsroom had already sourced and published. That is the error, and it is corrected here rather than argued.
