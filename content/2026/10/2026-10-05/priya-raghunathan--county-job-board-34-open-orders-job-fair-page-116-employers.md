OhioMeansJobs Belmont County, the county's own workforce center, is carrying 34 open job orders today. It is also running a job fair page that says 116 employers "still need employees and are hiring." Those two numbers come from the same office in Martins Ferry, and they do not describe the same set of jobs.

The live job-orders page is organized by category, 17 of them. Counting the orders on it as it stood at retrieval on Monday afternoon gives 34, carrying 33 distinct nine-digit order numbers. One number, 293522138, is printed twice. Northwood Health Systems appears under Health / Medical for a crisis stabilization unit nurse, and again under Social Services, where a single line carries seven titles, from community engagement specialist to day treatment technician.

Seventeen employer names carry those 34 orders. Wheeling Linen Service has seven of them, spread across an assistant plant supervisor, two production posts, a seamstress associate, a sales representative, a Class A CDL truck driver and a night watchman. Country Club Rehabilitation Campus has five. The International Union of Painters & Allied Trades has four.

Three of the 17 categories carry nothing at all. Business / Management and Maintenance / Mechanical / Repair print a heading and no order beneath it. Heavy Equipment Operation is the only category that says so in words: "No current job postings listed in this category."

The county's own instructions are candid about the catch. The page sends a reader to OhioMeansJobs.com by way of the job title, then says: "If a job link does not work, please call 1-740-579-0379 for assistance." It adds that "For many jobs posted on the local website, you may need to call OhioMeansJobs Belmont County to obtain application instructions," and closes by asking readers to "check this page frequently, as new job postings may be added daily or weekly."

The job fair page is the larger claim, and it is the one a reader cannot act on from the screen. It says the office "held its annual job fair on May 7th," and that "The employers listed below still need employees and are hiring." Under that sentence it lists 102 businesses, seven agencies and seven training providers — 116 entries — and directs anyone who wants to apply to the office at (740) 579-0379 or 302 Walnut Street in Martins Ferry. All three sections open with the same line: "This list will be updated as registrations are received."

The two lists barely meet. Six of the 17 employer names on the job-orders page also appear on the fair page: Kay Jewelers, Product Connections, Northwood Health Systems, Wheeling Linen Service — spelled "Services" there — Chick-fil-a, listed on the fair page as "Chick-fil-A St. Clairsville," and Belmont College, which sits in the training section rather than among the businesses. The other eleven appear on the fair page not at all.

Nor is the fair list a Belmont County list. Among the 102 businesses are the PA Department of Corrections, the WV Division of Corrections and Rehabilitation, a Jefferson County department of job and family services, a Wheeling fire department, a Wheeling police department and three branches of the United States military.

What neither page gives a reader is what a job seeker came for. There is no wage on either page. There is no hour, no shift and no posting date on either page. Neither page carries a ZIP code. The street address is on the job-fair page only, not on the job-orders page. The office hours exist, on the center's home page rather than on the two job pages: Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4 p.m., 302 Walnut Street, Martins Ferry, Ohio 43935, the same telephone number.

Two entries are thinner than the rest of the list. The Ohio Department of Transportation is the only business on the fair page listed with no industry line. Primerica is the only one listed without positions; what it carries instead is "Marketing wide range of financial services."

What the board does show is concentration. Twenty-one of the 34 orders come from four employers and one union: Wheeling Linen Service with seven, Country Club Rehabilitation with six — five printed as Campus and the registered-nurse line as Center — the International Union of Painters & Allied Trades with four, Northwood Health Systems with two and Chick-fil-a with two. The other 13 sit with 11 employers posting one or two apiece.

As of Monday afternoon, the board is the county's own, and it is the more useful of the two pages: it carries order numbers a reader can look up, while the fair page carries names and a telephone number but no order number a reader can look up. Read closely, the two say the county's employers are hiring, that 21 of the 34 open orders sit with five hiring entities, and that the honest next step for most of the 116 names on the fair page is a phone call.
